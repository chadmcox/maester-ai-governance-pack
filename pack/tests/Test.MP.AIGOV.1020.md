Agents and tools nobody uses still hold their access and connections, still appear to users, and still need patching and review. Removing them cuts your attack surface and the governance work with no loss to the business.

#### Remediation action

Confirm with the owner that the agent or tool isn't needed, then block it in the Microsoft 365 admin center
(**Agents > All agents**) or remove it.
