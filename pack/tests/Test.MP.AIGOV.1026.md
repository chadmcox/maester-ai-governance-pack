Budget alerts are how you find out a spending policy is close to its limit before users are blocked or costs climb. An alert with no working recipients, or only one person, can easily go unseen.

#### Remediation action

Edit the policy in **Copilot > Cost management > Configuration**, turn on an email alert (80% is a good
starting threshold) and add at least two active owners as recipients. Replace recipients who have left.
