A sponsor is the person accountable for an agent's purpose. When every sponsor has left or been disabled, nobody reviews the agent, approves its access or retires it, but it keeps working with its permissions.

#### Remediation action

Assign an active sponsor to each agent identity, or disable it. To stop this recurring, add the *Transfer agent
identity sponsorships to manager* task to your mover and leaver Lifecycle Workflows.
