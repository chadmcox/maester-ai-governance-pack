The AI administration roles can change every Copilot setting, approve agents and manage agent identities. Held permanently or by many people, they're a prime target: one compromised account can change Copilot for the whole organization.

#### Remediation action

Make each AI administration role eligible in Privileged Identity Management with approval and MFA on activation,
remove permanent active assignments, remove guests and apps, and keep the number of holders small.
