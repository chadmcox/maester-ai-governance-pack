A custom or third-party agent available to everyone reaches every user's data through Copilot at once. Rolling out to a group first limits the damage if the agent misbehaves, overshares or turns out to be malicious, and gives you time to review it.

#### Remediation action

Scope the package's availability and deployment to a specific group instead of the whole tenant, and
confirm the rollout plan with the agent's sponsor before widening it again.
