A spending policy with no overall ceiling can't stop runaway costs. A misconfigured agent, an automation loop or heavy use can run up Copilot credit charges with nothing to cap them until the bill arrives.

#### Remediation action

Set a numeric `periodLimit` on the policy, sized to the tenant's actual budget for that audience,
instead of leaving it unlimited.
