Spending policies use groups to decide who can spend Copilot credits. Microsoft 365 groups, nested groups or empty groups can include the wrong people or nobody, so the policy may not cover who you intend.

#### Remediation action

Link a security group to the policy, add people to it directly rather than through nested groups, and
retire policies whose group is empty.
