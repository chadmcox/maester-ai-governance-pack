People in a spending group get to spend Copilot credits. Disabled accounts, guests and people who haven't signed in for months shouldn't be there: they waste licences and budget, and an unused account with spending rights is a target.

#### Remediation action

Remove disabled, guest and inactive people from the policy's group, unless guests are meant to spend
credits. Assign a working Copilot license to anyone who should use a licensed service, or remove them.
