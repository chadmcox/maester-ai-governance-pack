A blueprint published by another organization creates agents that act in your tenant. Without a verified publisher, you can't confirm who built it, so you may be trusting an unknown party with your users' data.

#### Remediation action

Confirm each vendor. Remove blueprint principals you didn't approve, and ask approved vendors to complete
publisher verification.
