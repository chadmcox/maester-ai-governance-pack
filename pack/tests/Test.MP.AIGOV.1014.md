The group behind a spending policy decides who can spend Copilot credits. Without at least two owners and a description, nobody is clearly accountable for its membership, and people get added or kept with no record of why.

#### Remediation action

Add at least the configured minimum number of owners to the group, and give it a description that
explains why it exists and what spending policy depends on it.
