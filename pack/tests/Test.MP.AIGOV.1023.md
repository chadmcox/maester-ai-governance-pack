Admins, reviewers and incident responders identify agents by name. Vague names and missing descriptions slow down reviews and investigations, and make it easy to miss or keep an agent nobody can explain.

#### Remediation action

Rename agents and blueprints so people can tell what they do and who owns them. Describe each blueprint's
purpose, scope and owning team.
