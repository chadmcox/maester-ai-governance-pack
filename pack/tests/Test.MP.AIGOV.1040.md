Copilot data controls, Restricted Content Discovery and agent knowledge controls rely on sensitivity labels. Without a label for sites and groups, you can't protect whole sites from oversharing, and duplicate names make labels easy to apply wrongly.

#### Remediation action

Turn on the *Groups & sites* scope for at least one label, apply container labels to the SharePoint sites and Teams
that Copilot and agents ground on, and rename duplicate labels.
